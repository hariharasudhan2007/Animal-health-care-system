User Stories

US-01: As a pet owner, I want to create an account, register my pet details (species, breed, age, and medical history), and manage my profile information, so that I can keep my personal and pet records updated in the clinic system.

US-02: As a pet owner, I want to view doctor schedules, consultation types, and available time slots, so that I can select and book veterinary appointments.

US-03: As a veterinary staff member, I want to record checkup details, update vaccination statuses, and log treatment histories for each session, so that the clinic maintains accurate medical records for each animal.

US-04: As a pet owner, I want the system to calculate consultation, treatment, and medication fees and track payment status, so that I have visibility into my medical expenses and bill settlements.

US-05: As a veterinary staff member, I want to access a dedicated dashboard displaying appointments, patient records, treatment plans, and health announcements, so that I can efficiently manage daily clinic workflows and patient care.

US-06: As a pet owner, I want to browse upcoming pet health camps, vaccination drives, and wellness workshops and register for them, so that my pet can participate in community health and wellness events.
Use Case 1

ID: UC-01

Name: Register Pet Owner Account & Pet Details

Actor: Pet Owner

Goal: Create a user account, add pet details (species, breed, age, medical history), and manage profile information.

Pre-condition: The Pet Owner is on the system registration/profile management page.

Main Flow:

The Pet Owner enters account details and profile information.

The Pet Owner enters pet details, including species, breed, age, and medical history.

The system saves the user account and pet record.

The system displays a confirmation that the profile and pet registration are complete.

Alternative Flow:

2a. The Pet Owner updates existing profile information or existing pet details.

2b. The system saves the updated profile/pet information and confirms the update.

Exception Flow:

1a. The Pet Owner submits incomplete or invalid account/pet information.

1b. The system displays an error message and prompts the user to correct the missing or invalid fields.

Post-condition: The Pet Owner account and associated pet details are successfully stored in the database.

Use Case 2

ID: UC-02

Name: Book Veterinary Appointment

Actor: Pet Owner

Goal: View doctor schedules, consultation types, and availability slots, and book an appointment.

Pre-condition: The Pet Owner has a registered account and is logged in.

Main Flow:

The Pet Owner navigates to the appointment booking section.

The system displays available veterinary doctors, consultation types, and open time slots.

The Pet Owner selects a doctor, consultation type, and desired time slot.

The Pet Owner confirms the booking request.

The system records and confirms the appointment.

Alternative Flow:

3a. The Pet Owner filters or views different doctor schedules and consultation types before making a selection.

Exception Flow:

3a. The selected time slot becomes unavailable or invalid during selection.

3b. The system notifies the user and prompts them to select a different available slot.

Post-condition: The appointment is booked and stored in the clinic schedule.

Use Case 3

ID: UC-03

Name: Record Medical Checkup & Vaccination Tracking

Actor: Veterinary Staff

Goal: Log medical checkup details, update vaccination status, and record treatment history for an animal session.

Pre-condition: The Veterinary Staff member is logged into the system and an animal record exists.

Main Flow:

The Veterinary Staff accesses the patient record for the active session.

The staff member inputs medical checkup details.

The staff member updates the animal's vaccination status.

The staff member logs the session treatment history.

The system saves the medical checkup, vaccination, and treatment history entries.

Alternative Flow:

3a. The staff member updates an existing treatment history record or vaccination record.

Exception Flow:

2a. Required checkup or treatment details are missing during submission.

2b. The system displays an error message and prevents saving until mandatory fields are entered.

Post-condition: The animal's medical checkup, vaccination status, and treatment history are updated and saved.

Use Case 4

ID: UC-04

Name: Manage Billing & Track Payment Status

Actor: Pet Owner / Veterinary Staff

Goal: Calculate consultation, treatment, and medication fees, and track payment status.

Pre-condition: Treatment or consultation details for a session have been logged.

Main Flow:

The system calculates total fees based on consultation, treatment, and medication items.

The system generates the fee breakdown and payment status for the Pet Owner.

The Pet Owner views the fee details and current payment status.

The payment status is recorded/updated (e.g., Paid / Pending).

Alternative Flow:

2a. Veterinary Staff views or updates the payment status directly for a pet owner's bill.

Exception Flow:

1a. Unrecorded or missing treatment/medication data prevents fee calculation.

1b. The system flags the missing details and alerts the staff to complete treatment logs.

Post-condition: The consultation, treatment, and medication fees are calculated, and the payment status is accurately stored.

Use Case 5

ID: UC-05

Name: Veterinary Staff Dashboard Management

Actor: Veterinary Staff

Goal: Access a dashboard to view appointment details, manage patient records and treatment plans, and publish health announcements.

Pre-condition: The Veterinary Staff member is logged into the system.

Main Flow:

The Veterinary Staff navigates to the staff dashboard.

The system displays appointment details, patient records, treatment plans, and health announcements.

The staff member selects an item to manage (view appointments, update patient records/treatment plans, or publish announcements).

The system updates and saves the changes.

Alternative Flow:

3a. The staff member creates and publishes a new clinic health announcement to display on the platform.

Exception Flow:

3a. An invalid entry or empty announcement post is submitted.

3b. The system notifies the staff member and prevents empty or invalid record updates.

Post-condition: Dashboard records, treatment plans, or health announcements are successfully updated and displayed.

Use Case 6

ID: UC-06

Name: Register for Pet Care & Wellness Events

Actor: Pet Owner

Goal: Browse upcoming pet health camps, vaccination drives, or wellness workshops and register to participate.

Pre-condition: The Pet Owner is logged into the system and event details are published.

Main Flow:

The Pet Owner views the list of upcoming pet health camps, vaccination drives, and wellness workshops.

The Pet Owner selects a specific event to attend.

The Pet Owner submits a registration request for the event.

The system registers the Pet Owner/pet for the selected event and displays a confirmation.

Alternative Flow:

1a. The Pet Owner browses event details without registering.

Exception Flow:

3a. Registration fails due to system processing error or invalid selection.

3b. The system displays an error message prompting the user to try registering again.

Post-condition: The Pet Owner is registered for the selected wellness event.
